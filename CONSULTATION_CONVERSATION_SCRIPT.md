# Online Consultation Conversation Script

## Case

- Sign in as Doctor: `doctor / 123456`
- Open `Online Consultation > Consultation Queue`
- Locate consultation `#3004`, patient `Michael Davis`
- Select `Accept`, then `Open Chat`

The same questions and automatic responses work in every ongoing patient consultation.

## Conversation

Enter the following doctor messages in order. The patient replies automatically after each message.

**Doctor:** Hello Michael, how long have you had these symptoms?

**Patient:** The sore throat started three days ago, and the cough began yesterday.

**Doctor:** Have you had a fever?

**Patient:** My temperature was 37.8 degrees Celsius last night. It is 37.3 now.

**Doctor:** Do you have any medication allergies?

**Patient:** I am allergic to penicillin. It caused a rash when I was a child.

**Doctor:** Have you taken any medication?

**Patient:** I took one paracetamol tablet last night, and it helped a little.

**Doctor:** Do you have any shortness of breath or chest pain?

**Patient:** No, I can breathe normally and I do not have chest pain.

After the final reply, select `End Consultation`. The completed consultation will appear in Consultation History and can be used to create a medical record.
